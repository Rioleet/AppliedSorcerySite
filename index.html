Welcome to the village

Every ailment
has a story.

In a village of animal neighbors, healing begins with curiosity. Use spirit vision to uncover strange symptoms, gather and grow elemental ingredients, and brew a potion that helps.

No combat. No damage. No ticking clock. Just a gentle daily rhythm and small mysteries waiting to be understood.

01 / Observe

Look a little closer

Spirit vision turns each organ and symptom into something you can investigate. Follow the clues and discover what your patient truly needs.

02 / Gather

Grow what you need

Wander through the village, collect ingredients, and tend garden beds connected to the elements. Let the day unfold at your own pace.

03 / Heal

Brew a little hope

Mix ingredients into always-useful potions and help each patient through a standalone story of folk ailments and guardian spirits.
